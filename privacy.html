Privacy policy

C90 collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it never connects to the internet.

Last updated 30 September 2026.

What C90 reads

C90 reads the music folders you add and the SD cards you allow, so it can show your library and copy music. All of that stays on your Mac and your cards. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What C90 stores

On your Mac only: your settings, a list of your albums so the app opens quickly, a cache of cover images, and the permissions you have given it for folders and cards. Deleting the app's data removes all of it.

What Apple may share with us

If you bought C90 on the Mac App Store or use TestFlight, Apple handles the purchase, and may share crash reports and usage statistics with us only if you have agreed in System Settings to share them with app developers. They don't identify you. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

If you write to us

We use your email address and what you send only to answer you.

Changes and contact
