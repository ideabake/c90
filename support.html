Support

Questions, problems and ideas are welcome at ideabake@gmail.com.

It helps to say which version you have (C90 ▸ About C90), your macOS version, and for anything about a card, its size and format.

C90 asks me to allow access to my card

macOS only lets an app into a disk you have handed it. When a card's light under Devices is amber, click Allow Access in the panel on the right, then Allow. Don't pick a folder in that panel: C90 needs the card itself.

It asks again every time I connect the same card

On macOS 26, cards formatted as exFAT (most cards over 32 GB) can't have that permission saved, so C90 has to ask each time. This is a limit in macOS, and C90 says so when it applies. Cards formatted as FAT32 are asked for once.

My music folder shows “Grant Access…”

C90 can't open the folder: it was moved or renamed while C90 was closed, or it is on a drive that isn't connected. Connect the drive, or click Grant Access… and choose the folder where it is now. Your albums stay listed meanwhile.

My card is read-only

macOS can read NTFS cards but not write to them. Reformat the card as exFAT (this erases it) to copy music. If the card has a lock switch, slide it up and connect it again.

Some songs won't play in C90

Ogg, Opus, APE, WavPack, WMA and DSD can't be played on a Mac without extra software. They still copy to your card and play on your player.

Can I try it without an SD card?

Yes. Turn on Settings ▸ SD Card ▸ Show disk images as cards, then make a blank image in Disk Utility (File ▸ New Image ▸ Blank Image, formatted as ExFAT). It appears under Devices like a card.

C90 asks me to allow access to a CD

As with cards, macOS only lets an app read a disc you have handed it. The first time you rip a CD, a panel opens on the disc: click Allow.

My CD has no names, just “Track 1”, “Track 2”

C90 names CDs from gnudb, a free community database, but only if you let it go online (Settings ▸ CDs ▸ Look up CDs online). Some discs aren't in gnudb, and some have no CD-Text of their own. Click Edit on the CD's page to type the names, year and genre and add a cover before you rip.

Where do ripped CDs go?

Into one of your music folders, each album in its own artist and album folder, so it turns up in your library straight away. Choose which folder in Settings ▸ CDs. A song only appears once it has finished, so stopping a rip or ejecting the disc never leaves half a track behind.

Does C90 change my music files?

Only when you ask. Edit Album writes the tags and cover you choose into the files, and zip downloads in your music folder are unpacked beside themselves. Syncing only ever writes to the card, and ripping only adds new files.
