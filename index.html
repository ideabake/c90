Music for your SD card.

C90 is a small Mac app for people who keep their music as files, still have a shelf of CDs, and listen on a player that takes an SD card.

Point it at your music folder and it becomes a library you can browse, search, play and tidy. Put a CD in and C90 rips it into that library. Plug in a card, tick the albums that should be on it, and press Sync. C90 works out the difference, takes off what you unticked and copies on what you ticked.

What it does

  • Rips CDs as FLAC, ALAC, AAC or WAV, tagged and filed by artist and album.
  • Reads FLAC, MP3, AAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, Ogg, Opus and more, and flags albums whose track info would look wrong on a player.
  • Lets you add your own tags to albums, to filter and search by.
  • Syncs whole albums or single songs to any card a player can read, and shows how full the card will be first.
  • Writes safely: a pulled card or a cancel never leaves a half-written file that looks whole.
  • Finds albums that are only on the card and copies them back to your Mac.
  • Works with no account and collects nothing. It only goes online to look up CD names, and only if you say yes.

C90 needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. It is on the Mac App Store.
